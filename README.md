# DKU ezPrint

DKU's official ePrint drivers don't work on macOS 27. 

**DKU ezPrint** is a small free app that fixes this. It sends your PDF straight to DKU's ePrint system, and you pick it up at any ePrint station like usual.

## What you need

- A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 2020 or newer) running macOS 26 or later.
- Your Duke NetID.
- A connection to the DKU network. If you're off campus, connect to the DKU VPN first.

## Install

1. **[Download DKU ezPrint](https://github.com/pomegranar/ezprint-dku/releases/latest/download/DKU-ezPrint.dmg)**
2. Open the downloaded file **DKU-ezPrint.dmg** (it's probably in your Downloads folder).
3. In the window that opens, drag the **DKU ezPrint** icon onto the **Applications** folder.
4. Open **DKU ezPrint** from your Applications folder.

> The first time you open it, your Mac may say the app "cannot be verified" and refuse to open it. 
> 
> That's because the app isn't registered with Apple, which costs money. Otherwise it's safe to open:

1. Click **Done** on the warning.
2. Open **System Settings**, then go to **Privacy & Security**.
3. Scroll down to the message about DKU ezPrint and click **Open Anyway**.
4. Enter your Mac password if it asks.

*You only have to do this once.*

## Print something

1. Open DKU ezPrint and click **Open…**, or drag a PDF into the window.
2. Choose a printer: **Ricoh, Black & White**, **Ricoh, Colour**, or **Lexmark, Black & White**.
3. Type your **DKU NetID**.
4. If you want, change the pages, paper size, one or two-sided, pages per side, or booklet mode. The preview shows how it will print and how much it will cost.
5. Click **Print**.
6. Go to any ePrint station and tap your DKUCard to release your print.

⚠️ **Only PDFs work.** To print something else, like a Word document or a web page, use **File > Print** in that app, click the **PDF** button at the bottom left, choose **Save as PDF**, and then open that PDF in DKU ezPrint.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Are you connected to the DKU network?"** Connect to campus Wi-Fi, or to the DKU VPN if you're off campus, and try again.
- **"The print server rejected the document."** Check that you typed your NetID correctly.
- **Your print doesn't show up at the station.** Make sure the printer you picked in the app matches the station you're at (Ricoh or Lexmark).
